About Straight 2D Chest

Straight 2D Chest is the English edition of Recta Al Pecho — in Cuban baseball slang, a fastball thrown right at the letters. That is how we ask the questions.

We sit down with the people who lived Cuban baseball: Serie Nacional stars, Team Cuba veterans, the players who defected and reached the Major Leagues, and the ones who stayed and were forgotten. The conversations are long, unedited in spirit and uncensored. Some were recorded in Miami, some in Cuba.

The interviews are recorded in Spanish. Every video on this site has an English audio track on YouTube, so English-speaking fans can follow the whole conversation.

Hear it in English
  1. Press play.
  2. Open ⚙ Settings (on the YouTube app: ⋮).
  3. Choose Audio track → English.

Who makes it

Straight 2D Chest and Recta Al Pecho are produced by Marrero Bernal Media LLC, an independent media company. New Spanish-language interviews premiere every Thursday on the Recta Al Pecho YouTube channel.
